Class Board Instrument

The class board turns an ESP32-S3 dev board into a small lab instrument: eight analog inputs, two ±10 V outputs, fast TTL lines, a trigger, isolated inputs and a low-field NMR transmitter and receiver. This page is about the housing I designed so the board can sit on a lab bench as one closed instrument. The firmware and the phone app come next.

The board

The class board is the shared platform we designed together in Workshop 2: two 180 × 100 mm boards stacked 11 mm apart. The front panel is the face of the instrument, with sixteen SMA jacks (AI1–AI8, AO1–AO2, TRIG, AUX, FAST1–2, TX, RX), an OLED, three status LEDs, a Qwiic socket, a module header and four screw terminals on its front edge. The main board underneath carries the circuitry; its rear edge has USB-C, a 5 V DC jack, the 7–18 V external input and the coil terminals. The ESP32-S3 dev board plugs in below it.

The housing

Every dimension comes from the KiCad files and the board meshes, not from a ruler. Three parts and a lid:

PartWhat it doesMade by
BaseFour 2 mm walls, corner posts with M3 nut pockets that open to the side, four columns the board stack rests on (its screw heads locate it), rear openings sized for the plugs, engraved names3D print, no supports
CoverSits 2.2 mm above the panel, just over the SMA hex bases, so the plug nuts reach the thread. A hood holds the OLED 14 mm up; two bumps clear the optocouplers; everything else is solid plastic over the solder tails3D print face up, no supports
Label inlayOptional white fill for the engraved labels, printed as a second colour3D print (AMS)
LidClear bottom; four screws and it comes off to reach the dev board's RESET and BOOTLaser cut, 3 mm acrylic

Putting it together:

  1. Stack the boards on four M3 × 11 stand-offs with eight M3 × 6 screws.
  2. Drop the stack into the base; the main board's screw heads sit in the column tops.
  3. Slide four M3 nuts into the upper pockets of the posts and screw the cover on (M3 × 10).
  4. Four nuts into the lower pockets, and the lid goes on the same way.

Files

One parametric OpenSCAD file makes every part. GitHub shows the STL files in a 3D viewer.

FileWhat it is
housing.scadThe model (OpenSCAD 2021.01). part picks assembly, section, plate, base, cover, labels, lid or coupon; the console prints the numbers to check
base.stlBase, as it goes on the bed
cover.stl + cover_labels.stlCover, face up; the labels file is the optional white inlay
coupon.stlTest print: holes 3.0–3.6 mm, an SMA thread hole, a nut pocket, a 2 mm wall
lid.dxfBottom lid for the laser cutter
tools/The scripts that render the pictures on this page

The model reads the board meshes from the class repository by relative path. To open it with the boards in place, use the copy in class-board-2026/docs/students/lihdong/housing/.
